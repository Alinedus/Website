alined: Design Intelligence Layer for Architects

alined is a design intelligence layer for architects. It captures how architects actually work: sketching freely, branching ideas, iterating, and preserving that thinking as structured, actionable data.

Product Status

alined is currently developing its MVP and preparing for early access with architecture studios.

Who We Serve

Our initial users are architects and architecture studios that need to iterate on designs with clients without repeatedly moving between separate tools and workflows.

About the Company

alined (Botaline Innovation Private Limited) is a B2B SaaS, early-stage startup founded by Reshma Ashok and Rithu BD in 2026, based in Bengaluru.
